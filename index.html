The circumference is the total distance around the outside edge of a circle. If you wrap a flexible measuring tape around a circular object, the measurement you get is its circumference. For example, if a circular object measures 20 inches around, its circumference is 20 inches. Circumference is commonly used when measuring objects such as: Tires and wheels Pipes and tubing Circular tables Rings and bands Cylindrical objects Round woodworking materials Circular parts and components The circumference is different from the diameter. Circumference measures around the circle, while diameter measures straight across the circle through its center. What Is Diameter? The diameter is the straight-line distance from one side of a circle to the opposite side, passing directly through the center. For example, if a circle has a diameter of 10 inches, the distance from one edge to the opposite edge through the center is 10 inches. Diameter is important because it helps determine the overall size of a circular object. It is also directly related to circumference and radius. The relationship between circumference and diameter is constant for every circle because their ratio is equal to pi (π). Inch Calculator Circumference to Diameter Formula in Inches The formula for converting circumference to diameter is: Diameter = Circumference ÷ π Or: d = C ÷ π Where: d = diameter C = circumference π = approximately 3.14159265 The important thing to remember is that if your circumference is measured in inches, your resulting diameter will also be in inches. For example, if the circumference is 20 inches: 20 ÷ 3.14159 = 6.366 inches So, a circle with a circumference of 20 inches has a diameter of approximately 6.37 inches. Circumference vs. Diameter It is easy to confuse circumference and diameter because both describe the size of a circle. The main difference is how they are measured. Circumference is the distance around the circle. Diameter is the straight-line distance across the circle through its center. For example, imagine a circular table. If you measure around the outside edge, you are measuring its circumference. If you place a ruler across the widest part of the table through its center, you are measuring its diameter. The two measurements are connected by π: C = π × d Rearranging this formula gives: d = C ÷ π This relationship works regardless of the circle's size. How to Find Area From Circumference The circumference can also be used to determine the area of a circle. Once you calculate the diameter, divide it by two to get the radius. Then use: Area = π × r² For example, if the circumference is approximately 31.42 inches, the diameter is approximately 10 inches and the radius is approximately 5 inches. Therefore: Area = π × 5² Area ≈ 78.54 square inches This can be useful when working with circular surfaces, materials, layouts, and other measurements.
